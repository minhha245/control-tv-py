╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                      TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS                                ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Audio Processing:
├─ Sample Rate: 22050 Hz
├─ Duration: First 30 seconds only
├─ Channels: Mono
└─ Format: m4a (from YouTube)

Chroma Extraction:
├─ Method: Constant-Q Transform (chroma_cqt)
├─ Hop Length: 2048 samples
├─ Bins per Octave: 12
└─ Output: 12-dimensional vector (one per pitch class)

Key Detection:
├─ Algorithm: Krumhansl-Schmuckler
├─ Method: Pearson correlation
├─ Profiles: KK_MAJOR and KK_MINOR
├─ Keys Tested: 24 (12 major + 12 minor)
└─ Output: Key, Scale, Confidence (0-100%)
